# Objective Function

A subnet's objective function is the goal that the subnet is continuously optimizing for, through
its incentive mechanism. As with any machine learning model, a subnet has an objective function it
is continuously optimizing, and everything the subnet rewards is meant to move participants toward
that goal rather than away from it.

References:
[Objective Function](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#objective-function),
[Anatomy of an Incentive Mechanism](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism)

## A Goal, Not a Task

The objective function is easy to confuse with the subnet task, but they answer different questions.
The subnet task is the work the miners perform, while the objective function is what the subnet is
trying to maximize by having them perform it. A task can be carried out well or poorly; the
objective function is the standard of good that decides which is which. The task is the activity,
and the objective is the point of the activity.

References: [Subnet Task](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#subnet-task),
[Incentive Mechanism](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentive-mechanism)

## Reached Through Incentives

A goal on its own changes nothing; a subnet pursues its objective by paying for progress toward it.
The incentive mechanism rewards miner behavior that advances the objective and withholds reward from
behavior that does not, so self-interested participants are pulled toward the goal the subnet cares
about. The objective function is the destination, and the flow of incentives is the pull that keeps
participants headed there.

References:
[Incentive Mechanism](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/resources/glossary#incentive-mechanism),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Specific to Each Subnet

The objective function is set by the subnet, not by the network as a whole. Each subnet is
optimizing for its own goal, so what counts as good behavior in one subnet can be irrelevant in
another, and a miner that excels under one subnet's objective is not automatically valuable under a
different one. The network hosts many subnets at once, and each carries its own objective rather
than a single shared one.

References:
[Anatomy of an Incentive Mechanism](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/learn/anatomy-of-incentive-mechanism),
[Understanding Subnets](https://docs.learnbittensor.org/subnets/understanding-subnets)

## Reader Boundary

This page defines the concept and does not state the exact objective of any particular subnet, since
each subnet sets its own goal and can change it. The durable point is the relationship: the
objective function is what a subnet is trying to maximize, and its incentive mechanism is what
rewards participants for getting closer to that goal.
